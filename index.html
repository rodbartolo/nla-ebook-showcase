How it works

One pipeline, from brief to classroom film

  1. Brief and level

    A short idea becomes a 14-page plan pitched at the right NLA reading level, with Maltese places, food, words and names built in.

  2. Locked look

    An art style is chosen per book, and character portraits and place studies are locked so every page draws the same hero.

  3. Story and pictures

    Prose is written in one pass and each page is illustrated and reviewed by an AI art judge for style and consistency.

  4. Checked for learning

    Maths pages are checked by eye: exact counts, equal halves and quarters, correct clock faces and change.

  5. Exports

    The finished book ships as PDF, EPUB and an online reader, with a "Try it yourself" question on the last page.

  6. Film

    Pages are animated with Higgsfield, scored, and narrated in English and Maltese, then cut with captions and on-screen numbers.
